# Discussion template

Copy the headings into `discussion.md` at the start of a session. Sections stay
in this order — a reader who opens the file cold gets current state first, raw
material last. A section with nothing in it keeps its heading and says so
(`_None yet._`); an empty heading is a question the discussion has not reached,
which is itself worth seeing.

`<!-- comments -->` are guidance. Delete them from the real file.

---

```markdown
# <Idea name>

<!-- Once filed: the epic link is the first line of the file. -->
> Epic: <owner/repo#123>

## Where this stands

**Status:** exploring | parked | dead | ready-for-epic | became-epic-#123
**Last session:** YYYY-MM-DD

<!-- Rewritten every session, never appended to. One paragraph: what this is
now, and what the next move is. This is the first screen and the part a later
coduo-epic reads. -->

## The idea

<!-- What it is, in the user's own framing rather than a tidied-up version. -->

## Why

<!-- The problem, and who has it. If this section is thin the idea is thin —
say so rather than padding it. -->

## Decisions made

<!-- Each one carries its reason. A decision without a why is worthless in six
months, because the future reader cannot tell a considered choice from a coin
flip and will relitigate it. -->

- **<decision>** — <because ...>

## Rejected, and why

<!-- The alternatives considered and dropped. This is what stops the same
argument running again from zero. -->

- **<option>** — <rejected because ...>

## Open questions

<!-- The frontier: what a later session resumes from instead of restarting.
Tag the ones that block the idea moving forward. -->

- <question>

## Risks / unknowns

- <risk> — <what would tell us>

## Next steps

<!-- Free text carries the thought; the trailing skill name makes it
actionable when nobody remembers which tool does market research. -->

- [ ] <what needs doing> — </skill-name, if one fits>

## Adversarial take — YYYY-MM-DD

<!-- The sub-agent's critique, unfiltered, verbatim. Then the user's reaction
beneath it. Do not filter it down to what was accepted — a rejected objection
is the thing worth rereading when the idea changes shape. -->

> <critique>

**Reaction:** <what was accepted, what was not, and why>

## Session log

<!-- Appended live as sub-topics close. Rough is correct here: this is the
material Phase 2 promotes upward, and it stays after promotion. -->

### YYYY-MM-DD

- <note>

## In your words

<!-- The message that opened the discussion, verbatim, always. Plus anything
later that was explicitly flagged to keep. Blockquoted, dated. Never the whole
transcript — that is the original problem relocated. -->

> **YYYY-MM-DD** — <verbatim>
```
